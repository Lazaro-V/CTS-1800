FAQ — Quick Answers

What happens after the first year of free tutoring?

After the promotional year, families can choose a subscription or pay-as-you-go plan. We'll show simple pricing options in your account before you commit — no surprises.

How do I cancel or reschedule a session?

You can reschedule or cancel through your KidCode account. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice for live sessions so tutors can rebook; emergency changes are handled case-by-case.

How do you check tutors?

All tutors go through KidCode verification: ID check, professional references, and a background screen. Tutors also complete KidCode training so they're ready to work with kids.

What tech do we need for online lessons?

A stable internet connection and a tablet or laptop with a browser are enough. For video sessions we recommend a webcam and headphones to reduce distractions.

Is KidCode only for very young kids?

Nope — while our main audience is ages 5–7, older kids and teens can use the site too. Everything is written simply so families and first-time visitors aren't confused.

Do you share personal info?

We never share personal info without permission. Tutor contact details are only visible in your account after you approve a match. Read our privacy notes (link in the footer) for more details.
