About KidCode

KidCode creates playful, age-appropriate coding experiences for children ages 5–7. We focus on visual, hands-on learning that builds logical thinking and confidence.

Our Approach

Lessons are built as short, goal-driven activities with animated feedback and immediate rewards. We emphasize pattern recognition, sequencing, and simple loops.

Educators & Parents

We provide printable activities, progress trackers, and suggestions for offline extensions to reinforce concepts.
